# Event Report: Tech & Soft Skills Sharing Session - First Cloud AI Journey

### Event Details

* **Event Name:** Tech & Soft Skills Sharing Session - First Cloud AI Journey
* **Date & Time:** 09:00 - 12:00, June 13, 2026
* **Location:** AWS Vietnam Office, 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City
* **Role:** Attendee

---

### Main Content

During the sharing session, I received valuable insights from industry experts regarding personal development, technical skills, and corporate work culture:

#### 1. Soft Skills & Self-Motivation (Speaker: Mr. Nghi)
* **Inspirational message:** Overcome your fears and stay positive to grab opportunities when they arise.
* **Life lesson:** Always believe in yourself, with practical examples drawn from the speaker's own career journey.

#### 2. Practical Project Demo (Speakers: Mr. Kien & Mr. Hieu)
* **Technology showcase:** A live demo of a cloud project integrating key AWS services:
  * **Amazon CloudFront:** A content delivery network (CDN) service that accelerates website loading and reduces latency.
  * **Amazon WAF (Web Application Firewall):** Protects web applications against common web exploits.
  * **AWS Amplify:** A platform that simplifies building full-stack web and mobile applications.

#### 3. Data Analysis & Recruitment Skills (Speaker: Mr. Victor)
* **Data Analysis field:** Overview of career paths and opportunities in data analytics.
* **Interview skills:** Best practices for communicating effectively with recruiters.
* **Communication mindset:** When providing info, always put yourself in the shoes of the requester (recruiter/client) to understand their exact needs and deliver high-value answers.

#### 4. AWS Work Culture (Speaker: Mr. Nguyen Manh Cuong)
* **AWS Philosophy:** Deep dive into the unique working culture at Amazon/AWS:
  * **"It's only Day 1":** Always treat every day as the first day to maintain a startup spirit, stay curious, and constantly innovate.
  * **"Customer Obsession":** Keep customer value at the center of all technical and business decisions.
  * **Meaningful:** Focus on creating solutions that bring real, meaningful impact.

#### 5. DevOps Career Insights (Speaker: Mr. Truong Hoang Trong)
* **DevOps Overview:** Insights into the daily tasks, roles, and responsibilities of a DevOps engineer, bridging the gap between development and operations.

---

### Lessons Learned & Personal Takeaways

* **Mindset & Motivation:** Self-confidence and a proactive attitude are key to navigating and succeeding in the tech industry.
* **Communication:** Apply the user-centric mindset in technical discussions and interviews to deliver clear, relevant, and constructive answers.
* **Technical Knowledge:** Gained a better understanding of building full-stack architectures (Amplify) integrated with security (WAF) and optimization (CloudFront) in production-ready systems.
* **Work Culture:** Adopt AWS’s "Day 1" spirit and customer-obsessed mindset, applying these principles directly to my own personal Workshop project.
