During my internship at **Amazon Web Services Vietnam Co., Ltd.** from **11/06/2026** to **15/08/2026**, I had the opportunity to learn, practice, and apply the knowledge acquired in school to a real-world working environment.  

I participated in **studying and learning office work ethic as well as the current technologies of AWS web services**, through which I improved and consolidated critical skills including:
*   **Professional & Technical Skills:** Setting up containerized environments with Docker, configuring Web Servers and Reverse Proxies (Nginx), integrating storage services (Amazon S3), deploying virtualization infrastructure (EC2), setting up DNS routing (Route 53), and establishing log monitoring and automated error alerts (CloudWatch, SNS). Integrating AI/OCR solutions into a production stack.
*   **Problem-Solving & Self-Learning:** Embodying AWS's "Day 1" mentality (always maintaining the curiosity and drive of the first day) and a "Growth Mindset" to persist through complex technical challenges (such as configuring SSL or solving database OOM crashes with Swap memory allocation).

In terms of work ethic, I always strived to complete tasks well, complied with workplace regulations, and actively engaged with colleagues to improve work efficiency.
